# Dodorithmic — SFX Masterlist

Theme: Pixel / Arcade chiptune
Format: .mp3

---

## Music

music_menu (slow mysterious space chiptune, ambient synth loop) -
music_story (emotional minor-key chiptune, slower tempo) -
music_level1 (upbeat exploratory chiptune loop) -
music_level2 (slightly more urgent than level1, same arcade style) -
music_minigame (tense focused "thinking music" loop) -
music_gamecomplete (triumphant celebratory chiptune loop) -

---

## SFX

sfx_ui_click (crisp 8-bit click, used for all buttons, dialogue advance, press E) -
sfx_ui_hover (very short high-pitched blip on hover) -
sfx_transition (chiptune sweep/whoosh for any scene change) -
sfx_panel_open (ascending arpeggio power-on, used for dialogue box, puzzle tablet, archive) -
sfx_panel_close (descending power-down sweep, reverse of panel_open) -
sfx_dialogue_blip (tiny soft bleep, plays per character as text types out) -
sfx_interact_prompt (soft two-note chime when "Press E" appears near NPC or obstacle) -
sfx_player_footstep (short dull pixel tap, alternates per step) -
sfx_player_jump (bouncy boing, rising pitch 8-bit spring) -
sfx_player_double_jump (lighter fluttery version of jump, higher pitch) -
sfx_player_land (soft percussive thud with pixel crunch) -
sfx_puzzle_correct (positive ascending chime, rewarding and bright) -
sfx_puzzle_incorrect (descending buzzer bloorp, clear "nope") -
sfx_puzzle_complete (short 3-5 note triumphant fanfare, also used for algorithm unlocked toast) -
sfx_portal_hum (low pulsing drone, seamless loop while exit portal is visible) -
sfx_level_complete (stage clear jingle, plays when level is cleared) -
sfx_gamecomplete_sting (big one-shot fanfare, plays once on game complete screen) -
